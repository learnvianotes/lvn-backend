Exploring architectures powering image, audio, and video generation.

> [!NOTE] Diffusion Models
>
> Generates images by learning to denoise a starting image of pure random Gaussian noise. <br>- <strong>Forward Process</strong>: Iteratively adds noise to a real image until it becomes pure noise (analytic, no training). <br>- <strong>Reverse Process</strong>: A neural network (a U-Net, or in newer models a Diffusion Transformer (DiT)) is trained to predict the noise added at each step, allowing it to subtract noise iteratively to recover a clean, generated image.
>
> Stable Diffusion · DALL·E · Latent Diffusion

> [!NOTE] GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)
>
> A game-theoretic framework with two neural networks competing: <br>- <strong>Generator</strong>: Tries to create fake data (images) that look real. <br>- <strong>Discriminator</strong>: Tries to distinguish between real images and fake images generated by the Generator. <br>They train simultaneously in a minimax loop, driving the Generator to output highly realistic details.
>
> StyleGAN · CycleGAN · Minimax Loss
