This guide covers everything required to pass GenAI interviews, structured in three progressive tiers. Master core vocabulary, system architectures, and production scaling scenarios.

> [!NOTE] Level 1 — Easy
>
> <strong>Goal:</strong> "I know what every buzzword means."<br>Covers AI/ML/DL basics, basic NLP, tokenization, parameters, context, and prompts. Answers 50-60% of basic questions under a 1-minute read strategy.

> [!NOTE] Level 2 — Medium
>
> <strong>Goal:</strong> "I know how modern GenAI works."<br>Dives into Transformer internals (self-attention), GPT (decoder-only) vs BERT (encoder-only), embeddings, RAG pipeline, prompt engineering patterns, and fine-tuning techniques (LoRA).

> [!NOTE] Level 3 — Hard
>
> <strong>Goal:</strong> "I can design GenAI systems."<br>Covers LLM APIs vs local, agents/multi-agent orchestration, vector search &amp; indexing, diffusion/GAN architectures, latency/cost optimizations, and production guardrails.
