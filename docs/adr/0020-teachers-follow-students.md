# ADR-0020: Teachers can follow students

- Status: Accepted (owner decision, 2026-10-08)
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Spec sections: §5
- Amends: ADR-0016 (teachers did not follow students)

## Decision

Anyone signed in and verified — students and teachers — can follow a **student**, exactly as they follow teachers.
Unchanged safeguards from ADR-0016: never a **restricted minor**; following shows **PUBLIC** content only; only the
student sees who follows them; the student can **remove a follower, restrict or block** at any time, silently.

## Consequences

Teachers can keep up with students' public notes and projects outside a classroom. Every follow is one-way and
grants no access, so the safety model does not change.
