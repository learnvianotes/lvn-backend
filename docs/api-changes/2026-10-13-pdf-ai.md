# PDF pages read again, and AI clean-up (P5f)

- Date: 2026-10-13
- Package: `@lvn/contracts` 0.38.0
- Spec: §10.1, §18; ADR-0063 (stages 4 and 6 of ADR-0028)
- Breaking: no

## What changes for clients

- **`PdfConversion` gains `aiPages` and `aiRejected`** (both `Int`, null before the conversion's cards are made or when
  no page needed it):
  - `aiPages`: pages the vision model read again, where the OCR was unsure or met maths. At most about 15 % of the
    document's pages.
  - `aiRejected`: of those, readings that disagreed sharply with the OCR, so the OCR's cards were kept.
  - With AI off both are 0, and the conversion is unchanged.
  - Pages read again have cards of the usual types, anchored to their page as before.
- **`AiTask` gains `CLEAN_UP`.** It works on a note the person may edit (FORBIDDEN otherwise) and reads its text,
  heading and quote cards, up to 48,000 characters (`truncated` past that). VALIDATION when there are none.
- **New on `AiArtifact`: `changes: [AiChange!]`**, set for `CLEAN_UP` once it is READY.
  - Each change has `cardId`, `before` (what the card said), `after`, and `applied`.
  - The list is empty when nothing needs mending.
  - Only repairs are proposed. Changes that drop or add words are filtered out.
- **New mutation: `applyAiChanges(input: { artifactId, cardIds })`** applies the chosen changes.
  - It goes through the normal save; a card's type, level and anchor are kept.
  - It returns `applied`, the cards changed. A card edited since the change was made is not changed and is missing from
    `applied`.
  - `aiArtifact.changes[].applied` records what was applied.
  - NOT_FOUND for someone else's result; VALIDATION when none of the cards has a change.

## What clients should do

- **Conversion status:** when `aiPages` > 0, say how many pages AI read again, and how many of those readings were set
  aside.
- **"Clean up" for converted notes:** on a note the person may edit, offer CLEAN_UP (most useful on notes made from
  PDFs).
  - Show the changes as before and after, each chosen by default.
  - Apply the chosen ones with `applyAiChanges`, then refresh the note: the changed cards come back with new
    revisions.
  - Say which changes could not be applied because the card was edited meanwhile.
