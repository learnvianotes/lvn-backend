# Groups (Phase 3, P3c)

- Date: 2026-10-12
- Package: `@lvn/contracts` 0.21.0 plus events `GroupCreated` and `GroupDeleted`, and `GroupJoinRequested.reviewerIds`
- Spec: §4.1, §4.2, §5; ADR-0009, ADR-0011, ADR-0045
- Breaking: no

## What changes for clients

**Queries:**
- `group(id)`: a group the viewer may know of. That is every PUBLIC group, and any group whose link they have.
- `myGroups`, and `discoverGroups(query, first)`, which lists PUBLIC groups only.

**`Group`:**
- Always: `name`, `description`, `privacy`, `joinPolicy` (a PRIVATE group shows APPROVAL whatever was stored),
  `owner`, `memberCount`, `viewerRole`, `viewerRequestId` and `viewerCan { join requestJoin post moderate manage
  delete reviewRequests leave }`.
- Members only: `members`, `posts(first, after)` (with `pinned` on the first page) and `sharedNotes(first, after)`.
- Admins only: `joinRequests`.
- A field the viewer may not see is null.

**Mutations:**
- `createGroup`, `updateGroup`, `deleteGroup`.
- `joinGroup` (PUBLIC + OPEN only), `requestToJoinGroup(message)`, `withdrawGroupJoinRequest` and
  `reviewGroupJoinRequest(decision)`.
- `leaveGroup` (the owner hands over first), `removeGroupMember`, `setGroupRole` (below one's own role; OWNER hands
  the group over) and `muteGroupMember(until)`.
- `createGroupPost` and `editGroupPost` take `body`, a card's rich-text document. `deleteGroupPost` is for the
  author or a moderator; `pinGroupPost` is for moderators.
- `shareNoteToGroup` and `unshareNoteFromGroup` (notes): the owner, who must be a member.

**Refusals** come as FORBIDDEN errors in plain words: "This group approves its members: ask to join instead.", "You
are muted in this group for now.", "Share notes only with groups you are in.", and so on.

**Notifications:**
- `JOIN_REQUEST`, to the owner and admins: `actor` is who asked, path `/groups/<id>`.
- `JOIN_DECISION`, to the person who asked: `data.decision`.
- Both carry `Notification.group`.

**Blocks:** each person's posts are hidden from the other.

## Behind it

- `core.Group`, `GroupMember`, `GroupJoinRequest` and `GroupPost`. Every role, setting and membership change is
  audited.
- notes keeps `GroupRead`, `GroupMemberRead` and `GroupShare`, fed by core's events. The read predicate gains
  "shared to a group the viewer is in", for SHARED or PUBLIC notes with a published version. A test checks that the
  single-note rule and the list query agree for a group member.
- Plan limits `GROUPS_OWNED` and `GROUP_MEMBERS` (the owner's plan). With no row there is no limit.
- `comms.Notification.groupId`.
