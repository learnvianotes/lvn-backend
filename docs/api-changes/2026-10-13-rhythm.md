# Rhythm (P6d)

- Date: 2026-10-13
- Package: `@lvn/contracts` 0.43.0
- Spec: §14.2; ADR-0030, ADR-0069
- Breaking: no

## What changes for clients

- **`rhythm: Rhythm!`** (signed in) gives this week and the counters that never reset:
  - `timeZone`, `plannedDays` (ISO weekdays, 1 Monday … 7 Sunday), `target` (planned days less paused ones, at least
    1), `countedDays` and `met`;
  - `week`: seven `RhythmDay`s, Monday to Sunday, each with `day` (YYYY-MM-DD, a learning day in the person's zone),
    `weekday`, `planned`, `counted` and `status`: `COUNTED | MISSED | REST | PAUSED | TODAY | AHEAD`;
  - `today`: `day`, `learningSeconds`, `reviews`, `counted` (10 learning minutes or 10 reviews), `comeback` (the day
    after a missed planned day, until a day counts), `cameBack` (that day counted: back on rhythm) and `items`, the ring's parts (`REVIEWS` now; plan tasks and a
    weak topic arrive with the exam planner);
  - `weeksThisTerm`, `weeksAllTime` (met weeks) and `countedDaysAllTime`;
  - `pauses` (current and coming: `id`, `from`, `to`, `reason`, `active`) and `pauseDaysLeft` of the 14 a term.
- **`setRhythmPlan(input: { plannedDays })`** takes 3 to 6 distinct weekdays; others answer `VALIDATION`. The plan
  applies from the current week on; a closed week keeps the target it had.
- **`pauseRhythm(input: { from, to, reason })`** pauses from today or later, `reason` one of `EXAMS | ILLNESS |
  FESTIVAL | TRAVEL | OTHER`.
  - Past the 14 days a half-year term (January–June, July–December) answers `VALIDATION`.
  - Over another pause answers `CONFLICT`.
- **`endRhythmPause(input: { id })`** ends a pause early; one not started yet goes. Its unused days come back.
- The data export (`requestDataExport`) gains `learning/rhythm.json`: the plan, closed weeks and pauses.

## Authorization

- Each person sees and changes only their own Rhythm; signed out answers `UNAUTHENTICATED`.

## What clients should do

- Show the week from `week[].status`; never say a rhythm was "lost".
- Show a comeback on `today.comeback`, and refresh `rhythm` after a learning session or a review.
- Keep sending learning events while offline: a late event lands on the day it happened and closes its week again.
