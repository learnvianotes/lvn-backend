# Feeds (Phase 3, P3g)

- Date: 2026-10-13
- Package: `@lvn/contracts` 0.24.0 (unchanged)
- Spec: §3, §12.1, §13.1; ADR-0049
- Breaking: no

## What changes for clients

**`followingFeed(first, after)`** (notes subgraph):
- Returns PUBLIC notes and publications (every kind) of the people the viewer follows, newest published first.
- Items are `Note`s, read as their published version.
- Blocked people are left out, whoever blocked.
- Pages hold at most 50.

**`activityFeed(first, after)`** (core subgraph):
- Returns `ActivityItem { key kind at actor group classroom post announcement doubt note }`, newest first.
- `kind` is GROUP_POST, IDEA, DOUBT, CLASS_ANNOUNCEMENT or SHARED_NOTE. The matching field (`post`,
  `announcement`, `doubt` or `note`) holds the item, and `group` or `classroom` is where it happened.
- `key` is unique within the feed. It is deliberately not an `id`, so client caches do not merge a note shared to two
  places.
- Left out: the viewer's own doing, blocked people, and notes the viewer can no longer read.
- Pages hold at most 50.

## Behind it

- Both feeds are read at request time with keyset pagination. Nothing is stored or fanned out.
- `PEOPLE.followingIds` (at most 2,000) and `NOTE_ACCESS.placeShares` are new facade methods. Through the second,
  `notes` applies its read rule to notes shared to the viewer's groups and classes.
